01MSc research · Mobile ML

MalarAI

An offline Android app that screens blood-smear images for malaria parasites, built for clinics with no reliable internet. EfficientNet-B0 with Grad-CAM explanations, converted to TensorFlow Lite to run on-device.

  • Runs fully offline on an Android phone
  • Shows a Grad-CAM heatmap for every prediction
  • Designed for clinics in low-resource settings
  • TensorFlow Lite
  • EfficientNet-B0
  • Grad-CAM
  • Android
